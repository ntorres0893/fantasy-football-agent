## 🧠 Learn-with-me
**ADP is the price; your board is the value — draft the gap.** Notice today's
movers came from an **ADP risers/fallers** list. ADP (average draft position) is
just where the *crowd* is drafting a player — it's the market price. Your edge is
buying players whose *real* value (role, health, PPR fit) is higher than their ADP,
and avoiding the reverse. Example: Skattebo at RB18 is now fairly priced, but if he
were still going RB30 you'd have a bargain. On draft day I'll constantly compare
"where he's being picked" vs. "where he should go" and flag the gaps — that
discipline is the entire game of winning a draft.
