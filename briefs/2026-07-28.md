## 🧠 Learn-with-me
**Position label ≠ fantasy value — usage does.** Travis Hunter is a rare
two-way star, but if he plays mostly cornerback, he won't see enough *offensive
snaps and targets* to score like a fantasy WR — no matter how talented he is.
This is the core lesson of drafting: chase **role and volume**, not reputation.
A boring player with a guaranteed 20 touches/targets a game beats an exciting name
who's on the field for the other team's offense-stopping half of the game.
