## 🧠 Learn-with-me
**What a signing like Diggs does to a whole depth chart.** One veteran WR signing
ripples three ways: (1) **the new guy** gets a defined role (Diggs = WR2 value);
(2) **the incumbent alpha** (McLaurin) loses a few targets but usually stays
strong; (3) **the young guys** who were competing for that WR2 spot (Antonio
Williams, Luke McCaffrey) lose value and fall off the draftable radar. When you
see a signing, don't just value the newcomer — re-rank *everyone* around him. That
"who moved because of this" habit is how you stay ahead of a changing board.
