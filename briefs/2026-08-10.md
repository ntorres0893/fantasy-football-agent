## 🧠 Learn-with-me
**A minor injury to a late-round guy = wait-and-see, not panic.** Burden has a
"miss a little time" groin — for a *sleeper* you were going to draft late anyway,
that's not a reason to cross him off; it's a reason to hold and watch. Soft-tissue
tweaks in early August often clear up well before Week 1. The move is to keep him
on your "monitor" list and let his ADP dip while others overreact — then draft him
at a discount if he's healthy by your draft. Patience on minor injuries is quietly
one of the best value-generators in fantasy.
