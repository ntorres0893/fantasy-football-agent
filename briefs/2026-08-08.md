## 🧠 Learn-with-me
**Rookie hype vs. opportunity — the Jeremiyah Love lesson.** A talented rookie is
only as valuable as the **role** his team gives him. Love is a great prospect, but
Arizona just told us something by paying a veteran (Allgeier) and splitting first-
team reps: they're not handing him a bell-cow job — at least not yet. In fantasy,
**opportunity beats talent** almost every time at RB, because points come from
touches. So when a team signs a competitor for your rookie's job, that's a real
downgrade, even if the kid is the more talented player. Draft the *situation*, not
just the name on the jersey.
