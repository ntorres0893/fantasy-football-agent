## 🧠 Learn-with-me
**Why a settled QB helps the whole offense.** When a team's QB situation is
uncertain (holdout, competition, injury), coaches often simplify the scheme and
spread the ball around — which caps any one skill player's ceiling. Once the QB is
locked in (like Brissett now in Arizona), the offense can install its full
playbook and the top targets get a defined role. So "QB drama resolved" is a
quiet green light for that team's **best WR/RB**, even when it isn't headline news.
