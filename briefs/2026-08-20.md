## 🧠 Learn-with-me
**"Tightness" is a yellow flag you shouldn't ignore — especially on an older RB.**
Vague soft-tissue words like "tightness," "soreness," or "maintenance day" are how
teams *quietly* manage a nagging issue. On a 29-year-old back with a huge career
workload (McCaffrey), repeated tightness in late August is a real warning sign for
a body that may be wearing down. It doesn't mean *don't draft him* — it means
**don't pay the elite price**, because the injury risk that comes with those flags
lowers his expected games played. Downgrading a fragile stud a round is one of the
least glamorous but most profitable moves in a draft.
