## 🧠 Learn-with-me
**"Tightness" is a yellow flag — especially on an older RB.** Vague soft-tissue
words ("tightness," "soreness," "maintenance day") are how teams *quietly* manage a
nagging issue. On a 29-year-old back with a huge career workload (McCaffrey),
repeated tightness in late August warns of a body wearing down. It doesn't mean
*don't draft him* — it means **don't pay the elite price**, because those flags
lower his expected games played. Fading a fragile stud a round is unglamorous but
one of the most profitable draft moves.
