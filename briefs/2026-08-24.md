## 🧠 Learn-with-me
**Why the "whole team took a knee" detail matters more than the diagnosis.** When you see a description like that alongside a player writhing/not putting weight on a leg, that's the in-the-moment fear talking, not the medical result — teams don't know the real severity in real time either. The lesson: don't panic-drop a player off your board the instant a scary-looking injury happens, but don't ignore it either. Wait 24-48 hours for the actual diagnosis (as we just got with Jeanty: "sprained ankle," a real relief), then react to *that*, not the initial visual.
