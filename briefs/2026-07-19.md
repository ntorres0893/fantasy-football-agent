## 🧠 Learn-with-me
**Why WR is "safe" early but RB is "scarce."** Modern NFL backfields are mostly
committees — few teams give one back all the work — so quality RBs run out fast
(a "tier cliff"). WRs, by contrast, are plentiful and, in PPR, every catch scores.
So the winning move is often: grab a scarce elite RB *or* an elite WR early, then
**secure your second RB before the committee tier dries up (~Round 4)** while
staying patient on the deep WR pool. That's the spine of our whole draft plan.
