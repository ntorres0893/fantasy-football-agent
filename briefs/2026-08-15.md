## 🧠 Learn-with-me
**A team signing a replacement tells you how they really feel.** When Chicago
signed a veteran WR right after Burden's injury, that's a *tell* — teams don't add
bodies at a position they're confident about. It quietly signals Burden's Week 1
is shakier than the official "hopeful" quotes suggest. Learn to read these
**roster moves as coded injury updates**: a team trading for or signing a starter-
caliber player at an injured guy's position usually means the injury is more
serious than they're saying. It's one of the most reliable "read between the lines"
skills in fantasy.
