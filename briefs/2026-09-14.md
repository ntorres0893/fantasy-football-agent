## 🧠 Learn-with-me
**A single bad game (or great one) rarely tells the full story — but a pattern does.** McMillan's fumble is worth remembering, not overreacting to; one turnover in an otherwise strong 75-yard game is normal variance. What's worth tracking over the next few weeks is whether it's part of a pattern (ball security has been a real question for some receivers) or just a one-off in a blowout loss. Early-season overreactions to single-game data — good or bad — are one of the most common fantasy mistakes; let 2-3 weeks of games accumulate before adjusting your confidence in anyone.
