## 🧠 Learn-with-me
**Surgery news is different from a "questionable" tag — treat it as final, not as a probability.** Most in-season injury news is uncertain (a coach's optimistic quote, a limited practice designation) and worth weighing as a percentage chance. A confirmed surgical procedure with a named recovery timeline (Schefter reporting a meniscus trim, "a game or two") is about as close to certainty as fantasy news gets — there's no ambiguity to price in, so the correct move is to act on it immediately and fully, not partially hedge.
