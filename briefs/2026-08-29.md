## 🧠 Learn-with-me
**When a timeline gets extended once, expect it might again.** McVay initially said Nacua would be "back next week" — that week came and went, and now it's "back next week" again. This pattern (a moving target date) is more predictive than the injury name itself: a soft-tissue injury that keeps missing its own re-evaluation dates is a sign the recovery is slower than the team hoped, even if nothing is "serious." The lesson for draft day: weight your confidence not just on the diagnosis, but on whether the *timeline itself* has held up so far. A moved deadline is a flag, even without new bad news.
