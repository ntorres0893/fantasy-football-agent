## 🧠 Learn-with-me
**Why a QB's health lifts his receivers, not just him.** Fantasy points for WRs
and TEs depend on *who's throwing them the ball*. An elite, accurate QB like
Mahomes means more completions, more red-zone trust, and more garbage-time volume
— so his pass-catchers' floors and ceilings both rise. When you see "QB cleared,"
quietly upgrade his best receivers too. It's why Rice is a cleaner pick today than
he was 48 hours ago, even though nothing about *Rice* changed.
