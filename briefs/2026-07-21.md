## 🧠 Learn-with-me
**Why training-camp news is worth watching now.** Draft boards are built on
*last season* + offseason moves, but camps reveal what's actually true: who won a
starting job, who's healthy, who's buried on the depth chart. A player whose ADP
looks great today can crater by August if a rookie beats him out — or a late-round
guy (like a Jadarian Price winning Seattle's RB job) can become a steal. The daily
brief exists to catch those shifts before your draft, so your board reflects
reality, not June guesses.
