## 🧠 Learn-with-me
**When two sources disagree on an injury, that's information too.** It usually means one of two things: the team is being deliberately vague (common when they don't want to tip off an opponent, or the situation is still evolving), or two outlets are working from different, non-overlapping information (an older report not yet updated, versus a fresher one). Either way, the right move isn't to pick whichever story you like better — it's to wait for the next update, ideally an official practice report, which cuts through secondhand reporting with a hard participation status (Full/Limited/DNP).
