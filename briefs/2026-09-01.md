## 🧠 Learn-with-me
**Coach-speak is a real signal, if you read it precisely.** "We're counting on him" is a notably stronger commitment than "no timeline yet" or "week-to-week" — it tells you the team has already built its Week 1 plan around that player being active. It's not a guarantee (coaches can be wrong or overly optimistic), but it's meaningfully more information than silence. As you approach draft day, weight recent, specific coach quotes about a player's role higher than older, vaguer updates — the closer to Week 1, the more accurate team statements tend to get, since they can't afford to bluff much longer.
