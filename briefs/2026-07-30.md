## 🧠 Learn-with-me
**Why "full-speed in pads" beats any offseason report.** All summer, player news
is projection — coach-speak, rehab timelines, hype. Once pads come on and a
recovering star (like Nabers) practices *at full speed*, that's the first real
evidence, not a guess. It's also why smart drafters wait for camp: a name that was
a scary "injury fade" in June can become a confident pick in late July on one good
practice report. You're now drafting on evidence, which is exactly where you want
to be a few weeks out.
