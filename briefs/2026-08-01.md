## 🧠 Learn-with-me
**Injuries create opportunity — for other players.** When a starter like Pearsall
goes down, don't just cross him off — ask *"who benefits?"* A team may sign a
veteran (Deebo), promote a backup, or funnel targets to the next man up, and that
player's value can spike overnight. The same logic runs all season on the waiver
wire: the manager who reacts fastest to an injury — grabbing the *beneficiary* —
often wins the week. Reading the ripple effects, not just the headline, is a real
edge.
