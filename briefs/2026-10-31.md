## 🧠 Learn-with-me
**"Questionable" vs. "no designation" is the most useful line on the final injury report.** A player with no designation has been cleared, even if he was limited in practice. A questionable player is a real coin flip. When choosing between them, the cleared player usually wins, even with a later kickoff.
