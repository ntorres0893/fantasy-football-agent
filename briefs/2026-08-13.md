## 🧠 Learn-with-me
**Reading the FIRST official depth charts (out today).** Teams just released their
initial depth charts. They're written by PR staff and coaches love to downplay
them — but they're still your **first official look** at the pecking order: who's
listed as a starter, who "slid down," who's WR2 vs WR3. Combine that with *who's
getting first-team reps* in practice (the truer signal) and you can spot value
before your leaguemates. Tonight's games add a third data point. None of these is
gospel alone — but stacked together, they're how the board sharpens over the next
three weeks into your draft.
