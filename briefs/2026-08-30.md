## 🧠 Learn-with-me
**Why cutdown day resolves more than injuries — it resolves crowded rooms.** All summer we've tracked committees (ARI's RB room, SF's WR room, NE's backfield) where 4-5 guys competed for 2-3 real roles. Cutdown day forces those decisions: a team can't carry a 6-deep RB room into the season, so today the *fantasy-irrelevant* names get released, and the ones who remain have a clearer, more confirmed share of the work. This is different from an injury update — it's a permanent, structural answer. Read tomorrow's cuts as "who the team believes in," not just roster paperwork.
