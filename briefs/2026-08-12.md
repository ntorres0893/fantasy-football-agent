## 🧠 Learn-with-me
**"Monitor" vs. "move" — not every injury changes your board yet.** Josh Jacobs
has a groin issue and a vague "another week" timeline. That's a **monitor**: I flag
it, but I don't drop him down tiers until we know whether it threatens Week 1.
Overreacting to every early-August tweak is how people talk themselves out of good
players. The discipline is to hold your ranking, watch for the *next* data point
(does he practice this week? is he limited in the opener?), and only *then* adjust.
Same with McCaffrey's DNP — one missed practice is a flag, not a verdict. I'll tell
you the moment a "monitor" becomes a real "move."
