## 🧠 Learn-with-me
**What "PUP" means and why it matters.** PUP = Physically Unable to Perform. If a
player stays on the *regular-season* PUP list to start the year, he must miss
**at least the first 4 games**. So a "PUP" tag on Nabers isn't just noise — it's a
concrete signal you could lose a top pick for a quarter of the season. In a
10-team league where waivers are deep, that risk usually isn't worth a premium
pick unless he falls far enough to be pure upside.
