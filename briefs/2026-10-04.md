## 🧠 Learn-with-me
**A missing star WR changes his kicker more than his defense.** A WR1 being out lowers his own team's scoring, which hurts that team's QB, kicker and other pass catchers. His team's defense depends on the *opponent's* offense, so it's unaffected. Always ask which side of the ball an injury actually touches.
