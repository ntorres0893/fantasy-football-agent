## 🧠 Learn-with-me
**Not every "risk" is the same kind of risk.** Josh Jacobs' Commissioner's Exempt List placement was already a league-office decision with real teeth — he's out, no ambiguity. Nacua's situation is earlier-stage: a civil suit exists, the NFL is investigating, but no suspension has been handed down. The right way to price that difference isn't "avoid him" vs. "ignore it" — it's sizing the risk. A player under investigation-but-not-yet-disciplined is a discount, not a blackout; a player already on the Exempt List is a blackout, not a discount. Knowing which stage a situation is in changes how many rounds of value you should give up for the risk.
