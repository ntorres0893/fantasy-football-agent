## 🧠 Learn-with-me
**"Full participant" is the single most useful word in an injury report.** Practice status moves through a real hierarchy — Did Not Practice, Limited, Full — and jumping straight to full participation with contact drills (not just walkthroughs) is meaningfully different from a vague "trending positive" quote from a coach. When you're deciding how much to trust a recovery story, look for that specific word over general optimism — coaches can spin words, but practice participation reports are filed with the league and are much harder to fudge.
