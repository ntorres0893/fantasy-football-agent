## 🧠 Learn-with-me
**A backup's promotion to "RB1" is only real once a coach says it, not once a fantasy site guesses it.** George Holani is now *listed* as Seattle's RB1 — but that's a depth chart snapshot, not a guarantee. Depth charts this early in camp shuffle constantly as vets return from injury (Charbonnet could still reclaim the job once his ACL clears). Treat "RB1 on the depth chart" in late August as a strong signal, not gospel — the real confirmation comes from Week 1 snap counts. Draft the situation as "TBD, lean Holani," not "Holani is a lock."
