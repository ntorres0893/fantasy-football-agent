## 🧠 Learn-with-me
**"Avoided PUP" vs. "on PUP" — why the wording matters.** Regular-season PUP =
guaranteed to miss **at least 4 games.** So when a recovering player *avoids* the
list to open camp, it's a signal the team thinks he can practice and be ready for
Week 1 — a genuine value bump, because his ADP was likely still pricing in the
missed time. Nabers is the live example: same talent, materially better
availability outlook than a week ago. That's the kind of edge these daily checks
are built to catch before your draft.
