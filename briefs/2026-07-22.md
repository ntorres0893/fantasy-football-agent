## 🧠 Learn-with-me
**Why "padded practices" are the real tell.** The first few camp days are often in
shorts (no pads) — limited hitting, so RB/OL/DL evaluation is soft. Once teams put
the pads on, you learn who's actually winning the trench and goal-line work — which
is where RB fantasy value (carries + short TDs) is decided. So don't overreact to
Day 1 hype; the meaningful reports come a bit later. I'll flag them when they land.
