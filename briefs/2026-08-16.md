## 🧠 Learn-with-me
**Why Deebo is worth more in YOUR league than most rankings show.** A lot of ADP
lists still average PPR and standard together — but you're **full PPR**, where every
catch is a point. That makes a high-volume, dual-usage guy like Deebo (short
receptions + designed runs) more valuable *for you* than his generic ranking
suggests. This is the edge of knowing your exact scoring: when you see a player
whose game is built on **receptions**, bump him up a bit from the consensus, because
consensus is watering down what full PPR rewards. Draft to *your* rules, not the
average.
