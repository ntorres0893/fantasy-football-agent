## 🧠 Learn-with-me
**How to read "team interest" in free agency.** When a veteran WR like Diggs has
"5 teams interested" but stays unsigned, it usually means teams like the player
but not the *price* — so he'll likely sign a cheaper, incentive-based deal closer
to the season. For fantasy, the *landing spot* matters far more than the name: the
same player is a flex option on the Ravens (Lamar throwing) and nearly
undraftable on a run-heavy team. Wait for the destination before you value him.
