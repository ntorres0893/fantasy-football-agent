## 🧠 Learn-with-me
**Why an in-season-style trade in August matters more than it looks.** Teams don't usually make trades during preseason unless they're plugging a real hole — Houston giving up even a mid-round pick for Boutte tells you they're genuinely worried about their WR depth after Higgins' ACL tear. That's useful beyond just valuing Boutte: it confirms Houston's WR room *behind Nico Collins* is thin, which is exactly the kind of situation where an in-season waiver pickup (a rookie, a practice-squad promotion) can pay off fast if anyone gets hurt. File this team away as one to watch closely once the season starts.
