## 🧠 Learn-with-me
**Sitting out an entire preseason is a team's strongest possible signal of caution, not alarm.** Teams don't rest a franchise QB for a full preseason because they're worried he can't play — they do it because they don't need the reps to know he's their guy, and every unnecessary snap is unnecessary risk. The fact that Kansas City was that conservative with Mahomes' preparation, and is still on track to start him Week 1, is actually a vote of confidence in the recovery — the caution was in the ramp-up, not in the outcome.
