## 🧠 Learn-with-me
**NFI vs. PUP vs. IR — decoding the injury lists.** You'll see these all season:
- **PUP** (Physically Unable to Perform): football injury; on the *regular-season*
  list = out **at least 4 games** (Kittle's risk).
- **NFI** (Non-Football Injury): injury/illness not from team activity; similar
  early-season absence rules (Mixon's spot).
- **IR** (Injured Reserve): typically out a longer stretch; rules vary by when
  they're placed.
Knowing which list a player is on tells you *how long* you might be without him —
which is the difference between "draft at a discount" and "don't draft at all."
