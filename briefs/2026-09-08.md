## 🧠 Learn-with-me
**"Flared up" language after a positive stretch is worth taking seriously, but not panicking over.** A minor re-aggravation during a graduated return is actually a normal part of recovering from a soft-tissue injury — teams often deliberately push players toward full speed and then pull back when something twinges, rather than risk it in a real game. The distinguishing signal to watch for: did they immediately shut him down and reference imaging/further evaluation (worse), or did they just say "rest a few days and reassess" (still recoverable)? LaPorta's situation is the latter — a real flag, but not yet a red one.
