## 🧠 Learn-with-me
**Elite players survive bad situations — role players don't.** Notice how we treat
the two QB-adjacent worries differently: **Justin Jefferson** stays a first-round
pick despite Minnesota's QB question, because a truly elite WR gets *force-fed*
targets no matter who's under center. But a mid-tier guy like Shakir is far more
situation-dependent — his value swings hard on health and target share. Rule of
thumb: the higher a player's talent/role, the more he shrugs off noise; the lower,
the more every little cloud matters. Weight your worry accordingly.
