## 🧠 Learn-with-me
**"Missed Week 2 of preseason" vs. "may miss the opener" are very different sentences.** Love's story moved from a hopeful "should be fine for Week 1" to a real "might not play Week 1" in two days. That's the pattern with high ankle sprains — they often look better than they are early, then the real recovery timeline shows up once the swelling settles. Treat any high-ankle-sprain recovery estimate given in the first 48 hours as optimistic; wait for the 5-7 day follow-up report before trusting a Week-1 return.
