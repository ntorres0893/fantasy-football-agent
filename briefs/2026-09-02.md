## 🧠 Learn-with-me
**Whose word do you trust more: the coach or the player?** When a coach says "it's reasonable to assume" a guy plays, and the player himself says "I'm still checking boxes," that's not really a contradiction — it's two people speaking from different incentives. Coaches want to project confidence to the locker room and public; players (especially ones recovering from a serious injury like an ACL) are usually the last to declare themselves 100%, because they're the one who'll feel it if they're wrong. On draft day, when you get conflicting signals like this, lean slightly toward the player's own words — they have the most information and the most to lose from overstating it.
