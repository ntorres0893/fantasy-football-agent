## 🧠 Learn-with-me
**A legal/disciplinary absence is a different kind of risk than an injury.** Injuries have diagnoses, timelines, and re-evaluation dates — you can weigh confidence against how a recovery is tracking. A Commissioner's Exempt List placement doesn't work that way: it's a league-office decision tied to a legal process, not a medical one, so there's no meaningful "week-to-week" estimate to lean on. The practical rule: treat exempt-list players as a hold, not a hold-with-a-plan — don't draft them expecting a return date, because the NFL (not the team, not a doctor) controls when that clock starts.
