## 🧠 Learn-with-me
**How to handle an injury cloud on an early pick (Drake London).** You don't
automatically drop a stud for a *teammate's* injury — but you do adjust your
confidence. A shaky QB lowers a WR's ceiling and floor a bit, so London slides
from "slam-dunk early pick" to "still good, but I'd take a healthier option at
similar value if one's there." The skill is calibration: not panic-dropping, not
ignoring it — just nudging him a few spots and staying alert to updates. That's
exactly why we track these day to day instead of locking a board in June.
