## 🧠 Learn-with-me
**Why "who's behind him" matters as much as the starter.** A running back's
fantasy floor is mostly about **volume** — touches per game. A stud with a shaky
backup can lose goal-line and passing-down work; a merely-good back with *no*
competition (like Jonathan Taylor right now) can out-score him purely on volume.
When you compare RBs on draft day, don't just ask "how good is he?" — ask "how
many touches is he guaranteed?" That's why a thin depth chart is quietly bullish.
