## 🧠 Learn-with-me
**Why cutdown weekend is a mini-draft-season event of its own.** When a team cuts a player, it's not just that player's fantasy value that changes — the players *left standing* on the depth chart get real, confirmed roles for the first time. All summer we've been tracking "he's trending toward X job" language; cutdown weekend turns a lot of those into actual, locked-in roster spots. That's why your draft, sitting just 3 days after the deadline, is really well-timed — you'll be picking with some of the clearest information of the whole offseason.
