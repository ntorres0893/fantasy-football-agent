## 🧠 Learn-with-me
**The "punt the position" strategy — and why Kraft matters.** At TE (and QB) in a
10-team league, there's often a cheaper player with most of the elite guy's upside.
"Punting" means deliberately *not* paying up early, then grabbing that value later
and using your early picks on scarce RB/WR instead. Kraft is the textbook case: if
he hits his top-3 ceiling at a Round-8-ish price, you got elite-TE production
*and* spent your Round-3 pick on a stud receiver. That's how you win a draft on
value, not just talent.
