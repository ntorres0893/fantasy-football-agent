## 🧠 Learn-with-me
**Why a player like Deebo is worth a late pick in PPR.** In PPR, "touches" count
whether they come as catches or carries — and Deebo is used as a hybrid (short
receptions + designed runs). That gives him a higher **floor** than a pure deep
threat, because he racks up volume even in games where the big plays don't hit.
When you're filling out the back of your bench, prioritize players with a defined
role and multiple ways to score — they bust less often than one-dimensional
boom-or-bust names. It's the same "role and volume" lesson, applied to your last
few picks.
