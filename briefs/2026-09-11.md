## 🧠 Learn-with-me
**Friday's report is usually the last real update before kickoff.** By NFL rule, teams must file practice participation reports Wed/Thu/Fri, then a final game status (Out/Doubtful/Questionable, or no listing) by Friday afternoon for a Sunday game. Once that's filed, the volume of new information drops sharply — most "Questionable" tags simply play out as expected. That's why a clean Friday report, like today's across both rosters, is genuinely reassuring: it's close to the final word, not just another data point.
