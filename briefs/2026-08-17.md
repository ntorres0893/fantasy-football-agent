## 🧠 Learn-with-me
**Preseason SNAP COUNTS beat preseason stats.** Yesterday's Learn note said ignore
garbage yards — today shows the flip side of *good* preseason signal. Jeremiyah
Love's **16:6 snap edge over Allgeier** matters way more than any yardage total,
because **snaps = opportunity = fantasy points.** When you read preseason recaps,
skip past "Player X ran for 60 yards" and hunt for "**Player X led the backfield in
snaps / took the first-team reps.**" That's the coaching staff *telling you the
pecking order* — the single most useful thing preseason gives a fantasy drafter.
