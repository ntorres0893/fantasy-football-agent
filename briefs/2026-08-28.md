## 🧠 Learn-with-me
**"Taking it slow" from a team is a deliberate signal, not just caution.** When a team says they're *deliberately* slow-playing a star's return (like the Rams with Nacua), it usually means the injury genuinely isn't serious — they have no reason to rush a franchise piece back if there's real risk, so a calm, unhurried plan is actually reassuring. Contrast that with a vague "week-to-week" with no explanation, which tells you less. Read the *tone* of team statements, not just the injury name: deliberate patience on a clearly-not-serious injury is very different from cagey uncertainty about a real one.
