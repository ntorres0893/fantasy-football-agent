## 🧠 Learn-with-me
**How to read preseason games (starting tomorrow) without overreacting.** Preseason
stats are noisy — starters barely play, and big numbers often come against 3rd-
string defenses. What actually matters: (1) **who runs with the first team**,
(2) **usage/role** (targets, goal-line carries) more than yardage, and (3)
**injuries.** A backup torching scrubs in Q4 isn't a signal; a rookie taking
first-team slot reps *is*. I'll filter the preseason noise for you and only flag
what genuinely moves your board.
