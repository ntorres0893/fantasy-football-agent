## 🧠 Learn-with-me
**Where late-round drafts are won: backfield battles.** The top of your draft is
mostly settled — everyone knows the studs. The edge comes in Rounds 8–13, where a
back like **Brooks** or **Skattebo** can go from "handcuff dart" to "league-winning
starter" if he wins the job in August. That's why these camp battles matter: you
want to draft the *ascending* side of a committee (the guy trending up) rather than
the *fading* side, even when their current ADP looks similar. Watching who's
rising in pads is how you find those Round-10 gems before your leaguemates do.
