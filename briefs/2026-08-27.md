## 🧠 Learn-with-me
**Not all ankle sprains are equal — "low" vs. "high" matters a lot.** A **low ankle sprain** (below the ankle joint, ligaments on the outside of the foot) is the common, usually-quick-healing kind — most players are back in 1-3 weeks. A **high ankle sprain** (ligaments connecting the two lower leg bones, above the ankle) is the nastier one, often 3-6+ weeks, because that joint bears way more rotational stress. That's why today's news on Jeanty ("more low than high") is a real relief, while Jeremiyah Love's confirmed **high** ankle sprain is the more serious injury of the two — even though both got called "ankle sprains" in the initial reports. Always dig for that one word.
