## 🧠 Learn-with-me
**"Injury to Player A = draft Player B" — the substitution move.** Today's cleanest
example: Burden's injury doesn't just lower Burden — it *raises* Odunze, because
the targets have to go somewhere. Great drafters constantly play this
substitution game: every injury or depth-chart shift has a *beneficiary*, and the
beneficiary is often available a round or two later at a discount because casual
drafters only notice the *negative* headline. Train yourself to always ask "so who
gets those touches?" — that's where late-round league-winners hide.
