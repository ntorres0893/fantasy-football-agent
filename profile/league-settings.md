## What STANDARD (non-PPR) scoring changes vs. PPR

- **No points for catches.** Value now comes from **yards + TDs** only.
- **Pass-catching RBs lose relative value** (e.g., De'Von Achane, third-down backs)
  — their receptions no longer score.
- **Slot / possession WRs slide** (high-catch, low-yardage types); **big-play and
  TD-scoring WRs hold value** (Mike Evans, DK Metcalf, Nico Collins).
- **Pure-volume rushers rise** (Derrick Henry, Jonathan Taylor, Saquon) — early
  rounds lean **more RB-heavy** than in PPR.
- **TEs that live on receptions dip slightly**; the elite ones still matter.
- Net: **prioritize RBs a touch earlier**, and value WR/TE by **yards + TD upside**,
  not target/catch volume.
