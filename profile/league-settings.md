## Why these matter

- **PPR** heavily favors high-reception players — pass-catching RBs, slot WRs,
  and target-hog TEs jump up the board vs. standard scoring.
- **10-team** league = shallow. Talent is abundant on waivers; **stars win**.
  Reach for upside and elite ceilings; don't over-handcuff. Streaming
  QB/TE/D/ST/K is very viable because the bench is deep relative to demand.
- **Your draft slot** determines the entire round-by-round plan. As soon as you
  know it, tell the agent and it will generate a slot-specific game plan.
