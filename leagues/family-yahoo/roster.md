## ⚠️ Bye-week flags (found immediately — worth planning around now)

- **Week 11 — both rostered RBs share a bye.** Bijan Robinson (starter) AND
  Jadarian Price (your only bench RB) are both bye-11. With no RB-eligible flex
  in this league's roster format, **you will have zero startable RBs that
  week** unless you add one off waivers ahead of time. This is the single
  biggest structural issue on the roster — flag it again as Week 11 approaches,
  and start scouting a Week-11 streamer/handcuff a few weeks early.
- **Week 6 — Chase Brown (starter RB) and the Vikings D/ST share a bye.**
  Losing a starting RB and your DEF the same week; DEF is an easy same-week
  streaming fix (8-team league = deep waiver wire), but Chase Brown's slot
  needs Jadarian Price (bye 11, so he IS available Week 6) to cover, or a
  waiver add if Price isn't good enough that week.
- Other byes are spread out enough to not be a problem (Waddle/Odunze both
  Week 10 is a non-issue since Odunze's a bench WR anyway; Bowers Week 13 is
  covered by McBride sliding from flex into the TE slot that week).

## Roster construction notes

- **RB is thin: only 3 total** (Bijan, Chase Brown, Jadarian Price) with no
  RB-flex to lean on. In a non-PPR league this matters more, not less — RBs are
  already the premium scoring position here (rushing + rushing-TD heavy
  scoring, no receptions to prop up WRs). **Top priority once waivers open:
  add RB depth if anything decent clears.**
- **WR is solid (4 total)** — Waddle, McMillan, Odunze, Jameson Williams all
  rosterable in a pinch.
- **TE is excellent** — Bowers and McBride are both top-tier, one basically
  plays as a second WR/RB-adjacent scorer via the flex slot. Rare luxury.
- **QB has a strong QB2** in Prescott behind Daniels — not urgent, but a fine
  trade chip if you ever need to consolidate for RB depth.
- **Jadarian Price (SEA, RB)** is an unproven, low-profile name not currently
  tracked in `data/player-notes.md` — worth a quick research pass before Week 1
  to know his role/handcuff status.
