# 🧠 Draft Strategy — 10-Team FULL PPR (2026)

The philosophy behind the board. Read once so the live picks make sense.
**Scoring: FULL PPR — 1 point per reception.** Team: **Claude Me Daddy.**

## The big picture for a 10-team PPR league
- **Shallow league = star-driven.** Only 100 players start league-wide. Quality is
  abundant on waivers, so **draft for ceiling** and let the wire fix depth.
- **Every catch scores.** Value = yards + TDs **+ receptions.** Target volume and
  pass-catching roles matter a lot. High-catch players are worth more than their
  raw-yardage suggests.
- **Pass-catching RBs are premium; WR is deep.** The scarce, valuable early asset
  is a three-down RB who catches passes (Jahmyr Gibbs–DET, Christian McCaffrey–SF,
  De'Von Achane–MIA, James Cook–BUF, Kyren Williams–LAR). WRs are plentiful into
  Round 5–6, so you can be **patient at WR** and prioritize those receiving RBs —
  but elite WRs (Ja'Marr Chase–CIN, Puka Nacua–LAR, Amon-Ra St. Brown–DET) are
  also premium.

## Round-by-round framework

**Rounds 1–2 — Elite RB/WR only.**
- Tier-1 RBs (Gibbs–DET, Bijan Robinson–ATL) and elite WRs (Chase–CIN, Nacua–LAR,
  Jaxon Smith-Njigba–SEA, Justin Jefferson–MIN, ARSB–DET, CeeDee Lamb–DAL) are the
  pool. Pass-catching RBs (Achane–MIA) get a real PPR bump. No QB/TE/D/K (TE only
  if you want Brock Bowers–LV / Trey McBride–ARI).

**Rounds 3–5 — Build the RB/WR core.**
- Aim for **5 of your first 7 picks at RB/WR.** Lock a **pass-catching RB2 by
  Round 3–4** before the committee tier. The WR pool stays deep, so lean RB when
  it's close, but don't pass elite WR value.

**Rounds 6–10 — QB, complete the core, upside swings.**
- **QB:** 1-QB year — wait. Grab a Tier-2/3 QB here (Jayden Daniels–WAS, Jalen
  Hurts–PHI, Drake Maye–NE, Joe Burrow–CIN, Bo Nix–DEN, Dak Prescott–DAL). Only
  reach for Josh Allen (BUF) / Lamar Jackson (BAL) at real value.
- Add high-target WRs and pass-catching backs (PPR points come from volume).

**Rounds 11–13 — Upside darts + late TE (if punting).**
- Ascending backfield winners (e.g., Jonathon Brooks–CAR), target-earning
  rookies, buy-the-dip talents.

**Last 2 rounds — D/ST and K. Never earlier.** Stream weekly (kicker scoring here
rewards big legs; D/ST scoring rewards facing weak offenses).

## Archetypes that WIN in PPR
- **Up 🟢:** pass-catching RBs (Achane–MIA, Cook–BUF, Kyren–LAR, Chase Brown–CIN),
  target-hog WRs (ARSB–DET, Nacua–LAR, Ladd McConkey–LAC, Jaylen Waddle–DEN), and
  elite target-share TEs (Bowers–LV, McBride–ARI).
- **Down 🔴 (vs. their reputation):** pure-rush, low-catch RBs (Derrick Henry–BAL
  gives back a little without receptions) and TD-only WRs with thin target volume.

## The three viable builds
1. **Robust-RB / Hero-RB:** grab elite RB(s) early — pass-catching backs are scarce
   — then hammer the deep WR pool.
2. **Zero-RB:** very viable in PPR — load elite WRs (and maybe an elite TE) early,
   then attack RB upside/committee-winners from Round 5. Needs active waiver play.
3. **Balanced BPA:** best value by tier each pick — usual landing spot.

## TE decision (hard cliff)
- **Pay up** for **Brock Bowers (LV) or Trey McBride (ARI)** (R2–3) for a weekly
  edge, **or wait and stream.** Avoid the mushy middle. Value watch:
  **Tucker Kraft (GB)** (top-3 upside at a discount if healthy). George Kittle
  (SF) is 🔻 (PUP/Achilles) — don't pay up.

## Positional targets by round (PPR)
- **Anchor RB:** R1–3. **RB2 (pass-catching):** by R3–4.
- **WR1/WR2:** R1–5 — deep pool, be patient.
- **QB1:** R6–10 (stream-friendly). **TE1:** R2–3 (elite) or R10+ (stream).
- **Depth/sleepers:** R6–13. **D/ST, K:** final 2 rounds.

## Discipline rules
- Best value **by tier**; take the last player in a tier before the cliff.
- Prioritize **pass-catching RBs** early (scarce); stay patient on the deep WR pool.
- Don't overpay a pure-rusher's PPR ranking — his lack of catches caps him.
- Fandom doesn't pick players; the board does (override live anytime).
