## Key takeaways

- **Week 5 is the crunch week** — toughest opponent (Pigskin Pugs, Allen/McBride)
  lands the exact same week Mahomes is on bye. This is the single biggest
  reason to get a Week 5 QB streamer locked in early rather than scrambling.
- **Weeks 6 and 8 double up** an opponent's real strength against our own bye
  losses (Saleh's elite WRs in wk6 while we're down Achane/LaPorta; Entire
  Teams For Sale's strong RB room in wk8 while we're down 2 bench WRs — though
  wk8's losses are bench-only, so lower stakes).
- **Weeks 4, 8, and 9** face opponents with a real roster weakness (Cayenne's
  Nacua risk, and thin WR rooms for Entire Teams For Sale / Papacito) — good
  spots to lean into confidence if your own roster is healthy.
