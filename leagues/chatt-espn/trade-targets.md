# Trade Targets — Chatt-ESPN (Claude Me Daddy)

> Identified 9/3/2026 after the draft, cross-referencing our RB surplus (6
> rostered — deepest in the league) against other rosters' needs in
> `leagues/chatt-espn/league-rosters.md`. Our clearest need is QB depth
> (Mahomes only, no backup, Week 5 bye lands on our toughest matchup —
> see `leagues/chatt-espn/schedule.md`).

## Priority targets (best value — their unused piece fixes our real gap)

1. **Kyler Murray (QB, Pigskin Pugs)** for a spare RB (Roschon Johnson or
   Kaleb Johnson). Murray is dead weight behind Josh Allen for them; for us
   he directly plugs the Week 5 hole and has real rushing floor even in a
   timeshare. Best offer on the board — close to a free upgrade.
2. **Matthew Stafford (QB, Cayenne You Dig It?!)** for the same tier of RB.
   Backup plan if the Murray offer doesn't land — Prescott is their guy,
   Stafford is pure insurance sitting idle.

## Upside plays (not urgent, worth floating)

3. **Xavier Worthy (WR, HammerTime)** for Rachaad White (RB). They're
   rostering 7 WRs — Worthy is buried; our WR room is "5 solid," not "has a
   difference-maker." Low-cost shot at adding one.
4. **Davante Adams or D. Stribling (WR, Saleh's Super Team)** for a real RB
   (White or Hall). Bigger ask — their RB room (Love/Kyren/Henderson/
   Monangai/Dobbins) is a pile of injury/committee risk and a dependable
   back has real value to them; costs more than the QB deals but is a true
   lineup upgrade, not just a depth swap.

## Not worth pursuing

- **Papacito** — no good fit. Their surplus (RB) mirrors our own, and Jayden
  Daniels' injury history means Jaxson Dart isn't dead weight for them the
  way Murray/Stafford are elsewhere — they're less likely to move him.
  If forced to find an "even" trade: Boutte/Golden for Hubbard/Marks is fair
  value but low impact either direction. Better to spend trade capital on
  targets 1-4 above.

## Status
All targets above are proposals only — no offers sent yet as of 9/3/2026.
Update this file as offers go out / get accepted / rejected.
