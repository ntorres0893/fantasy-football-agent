## League landscape (all 10 rosters now known)

**Elite WR duos/trios already owned:** Chase+Nabers (Stinky), ARSB+Lamb+Waddle
(Saleh), JSN+Jefferson (Wildcard Lloyd) — three teams have serious WR stacks.
Our own Rice/Metcalf/Deebo/Golden/Boutte is more "5 good options" than "2-3
elite," which is fine in this deep WR format but worth knowing going into
trade talks.

**RB handcuffs/committee backs already owned** (fewer available on waivers
than it might seem): Jonathon Brooks & Cam Corum-type names (Stinky), Blake
Corum (Wildcard Lloyd), Zach Charbonnet (Hammertime), Kyle Monangai/J.K.
Dobbins (Saleh), Croskey-Merritt/B. Robinson Jr. (Jason Butts). Our own bench
already covers Achane/Barkley/Hall, so this mostly matters if we ever need a
Week 5 QB streamer and are scanning what's NOT already owned.

**Teams most exposed to Nacua getting suspended:** Cayenne You Dig It?!
(has him starting) — the only team truly at risk from that story.

**Teams with a dead/wasted roster spot right now:** Cayenne You Dig It?!
(Josh Jacobs, Exempt List).

**Our (Claude Me Daddy) relative standing:** best RB depth in the league
(Achane/Barkley/Hall/White/Roschon/Kaleb Johnson — nobody else has 6
rosterable RBs), competitive but not top-tier WR, and the QB gap (Week 5,
Mahomes bye, no backup) is a real weakness several other teams (Papacito,
Jason Butts, Cayenne) also share — so it's not fatal, but worth fixing early
since it's a leaguewide scarce resource once Week 5 approaches.
