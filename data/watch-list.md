## 🏈 QB — **wait; 1-QB league, don't draft early (target Rounds 6–10)**
- **Draft high ONLY at value (R5–6):** Josh Allen, Lamar Jackson — elite, but don't reach; someone else usually overpays.
- **The sweet spot (R6–9):** Jayden Daniels, Jalen Hurts, Joe Burrow, Drake Maye — dual-threat rushing = safe weekly floor.
- **Late / stream (R10+):** Bo Nix, Caleb Williams, Dak Prescott, Kyler Murray*, Patrick Mahomes✅ (*if he wins the MIN job).
- **Bottom line:** never take a QB before Round 6. Two mid-QBs late > one early QB.

## 🏃 RB — **the premium position in PPR; secure your core early**
- **Draft EARLY / Round 1 (elite):** Jahmyr Gibbs, Bijan Robinson · then De'Von Achane🟢, Jonathan Taylor, Saquon Barkley.
  - ⚠️ **Christian McCaffrey** — slid to **back of R1** ("tightness"); value ~picks 10–13, not top-8.
- **Rounds 2–4 (strong RB1/RB2):** Ashton Jeanty, James Cook, Kyren Williams, Derrick Henry, Chase Brown🟢, Bucky Irving🟢.
  - ⚠️ **Josh Jacobs** (groin, out ≥1 wk) — target only if he slides.
- **Rounds 5–8 (RB2 / flex):** Cam Skattebo🟢 (NYG lead), D'Andre Swift🟢 (CHI), Breece Hall⚠️ (poss. groin), Kenneth Walker III⚠️.
  - 🔻 **Chuba Hubbard** (hamstring) — fade a bit.
- **Late / sleepers (R9+):** Jonathon Brooks🟢 (Hubbard hurt), J.K. Dobbins (DEN RB1), Bhayshul Tuten, Rachaad White, Tyler Allgeier, Kenneth Gainwell, Jam Miller.
- **Avoid / fade:** Jeremiyah Love⚠️ (high ankle sprain — stash only), RJ Harvey🔻 (behind Dobbins).

## 🎯 TE — **pay up for the top 2, or wait and stream — avoid the middle**
- **Draft high (Rounds 2–3):** Brock Bowers, Trey McBride — elite target hogs, weekly edge.
- **💎 VALUE target (R7–9):** **Tucker Kraft** — top-3 TE upside at a discount if the ACL's fully behind him.
- **Mid (only if you missed the top 2):** Sam LaPorta, Tyler Warren.
- **Late / stream (R11+):** Colston Loveland, Harold Fannin Jr., Dalton Kincaid, Isaiah Likely, Hunter Henry.
- **Don't pay up:** George Kittle⚠️ (PUP/Achilles — Week 1 not guaranteed).

## 🛡️ D/ST — **last two rounds only; stream weekly**
- Don't draft before ~Round 13. This scoring rewards **facing weak offenses** (points **and** yards allowed tiers), so stream a unit with a soft Week-1 matchup rather than "the best defense."
- Grab one you like late; drop it if the Week-1 matchup is bad.

## 🦵 K — **last pick; stream**
- Distance-based FG scoring here **rewards big legs** (50–59 = 5 pts, 60+ = 6). Lean a strong-legged kicker in a good offense, but never spend more than your final pick.
