## 🛡️ D/ST — last two rounds only; stream weekly
- Don't draft before ~Round 13. Scoring rewards facing **weak offenses** (points and
  yards allowed), so stream a soft Week-1 matchup over "the best defense."

## 🦵 K — final pick; stream
- Distance-based FG scoring rewards **big legs** (50–59 = 5 pts, 60+ = 6). Lean a
  strong-legged kicker in a good offense — but never before your last pick.
